Why we chose this topic
WHY THIS TOPIC?

A better essay.
But whose thinking?

AI makes polished writing easier to produce. Our project asks what students still need to practise, understand and explain for themselves.

WHAT WE NOTICE AMONG OUR PEERS

Essay writing can feel like the hardest part.

We have noticed that many of our peers struggle with writing an essay, even when speaking, reading and listening feel easier to them. Turning ideas into a clear argument, choosing the right words and organising paragraphs can be especially challenging.

WHY WE WANTED TO HELP

Clear feedback. More confident progress.

We wanted to help them tackle these difficulties and make steadier, more efficient progress. Our goal is to offer clear explanations and personalised feedback so students can improve their own writing, rather than copy a finished essay.

This motivation comes from our informal observations of peers, not a formal survey or evidence that one skill is harder for every learner.

SAMPLE RESPONSE

Should students use AI when writing essays?

Example word count

Introduction. Artificial intelligence is becoming a normal part of education, and many students now use tools such as chatbots when they write essays. Some people believe that this makes learning easier, while others worry that students will stop developing their own skills. In my opinion, AI can be useful for writing, but it should support the student rather than replace the student's thinking.

Body paragraph 1. One advantage of AI is that it can give immediate feedback. For example, a student can ask it to identify repeated words, unclear sentences or common grammar mistakes. This can help learners notice problems that they might miss when checking their work alone. AI can also suggest several vocabulary choices, which allows students to compare different ways of expressing the same idea.

Body paragraph 2. However, using AI to create a complete essay can cause serious problems. If a student simply copies a generated answer, the final text may look impressive without showing what the student actually understands. The student may also find it difficult to explain the argument or vocabulary during a discussion. Therefore, the quality of the final text is not the only thing that should be assessed.

Conclusion. Overall, I believe the best approach is balanced. Students should develop their ideas and write a first draft themselves, then use AI to receive feedback and make informed revisions. In this way, technology can improve the writing process while human judgement, responsibility and learning remain at the centre.

08 / WRITING TIPS

Write smarter,
not just longer.

Practical advice for planning, structuring, revising and improving an English essay.

01

Understand the question

Underline the key instruction and decide exactly what your essay must answer. Do not start writing before you know your position.

Ask yourself: What is the question really asking me to prove?
02

Make a quick plan

Choose your main ideas first. A simple outline keeps each paragraph focused and prevents repetition.

Formula: thesis → point → explanation → example → link
03

Build strong paragraphs

Give every body paragraph one clear main idea. Explain your example instead of simply dropping it into the text.

Remember: Point + Evidence + Explanation
04

Use linking words naturally

Use connectors to show relationships between ideas, but avoid putting a transition at the start of every sentence.

Try: However · Moreover · Therefore · For instance
05

Revise in two passes

First check ideas and organization. Then check grammar, spelling, vocabulary and punctuation. This is more effective than checking everything at once.

Pass 1: meaning & structure · Pass 2: language
06

Use AI as feedback

Ask AI to diagnose problems or ask questions about your argument. Keep the ideas, decisions and final wording under your control.

Better prompt: “Find three weaknesses and explain how I can improve them.”

QUICK GUIDE

Frequently asked
questions.

Does Scribe AI write a complete essay for me?

The new coach is designed for feedback and reflection. It offers observations and questions so that you make your own revisions, rather than providing a finished essay.

Is the writing coach connected to an AI model?

Read the connection label above the draft field. If the service is disconnected or unavailable, no assessment is produced. When connected, submitting a draft sends it to the AI service for analysis. Band estimates are approximate, not official IELTS results.

Should students use AI when writing essays?

AI can be useful when it supports learning. The student should develop the ideas and remain responsible for the final work.

What is the safest way to use AI?

Use AI to explain grammar, diagnose weaknesses, suggest limited vocabulary options, or ask questions about your argument.

Why is a full AI-generated essay different?

A polished text can hide whether the student actually understands and owns the ideas. That is why originality and oral explanation matter.

What are the Green, Yellow and Red Zones?

Green means AI acts as a tutor. Yellow means AI becomes a co-writer and requires more caution. Red means AI replaces the assessed work.

What is the seven-step workflow?

Think → Plan → Write → Ask → Check → Revise → Defend. The key idea is to keep human judgement in control.

What did the pilot results show?

The independent, AI-assisted and AI-generated conditions scored 20/25, 23/25 and 20/25 respectively. Limited AI assistance had the highest total in this one-case comparison; it does not establish a general effect.
