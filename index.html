This project compares independent writing, limited AI assistance, and AI-generated writing using the same task and the same five-criterion rubric.

SAMPLE RESPONSE

Should students use AI when writing essays?

≈ 260 words

Introduction. Artificial intelligence is becoming a normal part of education, and many students now use tools such as chatbots when they write essays. Some people believe that this makes learning easier, while others worry that students will stop developing their own skills. In my opinion, AI can be useful for writing, but it should support the student rather than replace the student's thinking.

Body paragraph 1. One advantage of AI is that it can give immediate feedback. For example, a student can ask it to identify repeated words, unclear sentences or common grammar mistakes. This can help learners notice problems that they might miss when checking their work alone. AI can also suggest several vocabulary choices, which allows students to compare different ways of expressing the same idea.

Body paragraph 2. However, using AI to create a complete essay can cause serious problems. If a student simply copies a generated answer, the final text may look impressive without showing what the student actually understands. The student may also find it difficult to explain the argument or vocabulary during a discussion. Therefore, the quality of the final text is not the only thing that should be assessed.

Conclusion. Overall, I believe the best approach is balanced. Students should develop their ideas and write a first draft themselves, then use AI to receive feedback and make informed revisions. In this way, technology can improve the writing process while human judgement, responsibility and learning remain at the centre.
