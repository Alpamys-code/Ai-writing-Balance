01 / THE PROBLEM

Better writing doesn't
always mean more learning.

Generative AI can produce polished language. But a polished final essay may hide how much the student actually understands, created, and can explain.

This project compares independent writing, limited AI assistance, and AI-generated writing using the same task and the same five-criterion rubric.

04 / AI WRITING BALANCE

Not all AI use
is the same.

The model classifies the function AI performs in relation to student responsibility.

✓
GREEN · GENERALLY ALLOWED

Keep the student in control.

Use AI to explain a grammar error, offer vocabulary alternatives, ask questions about an argument, or identify unclear sentences without replacing the student's work.

05 / RESPONSIBLE WORKFLOW

Seven steps.
Human judgement first.

01THINK

Choose your position without AI.

02PLAN

Build the structure independently.

03WRITE

Create your first draft.

04ASK

Ask AI a specific question.

05CHECK

Verify accuracy and appropriateness.

06REVISE

Use your own judgement to edit.

07DEFEND

Explain and defend your final choices.

HUMAN IDEA+HUMAN DRAFT+AI FEEDBACK+HUMAN DECISION= RESPONSIBLE AI-ASSISTED WRITING
06 / PROMPT LAB

Ask AI to help you
think better.

Good educational prompts preserve productive effort instead of replacing it.

✓GREEN-ZONE PROMPT
“Identify up to three grammar problems in this paragraph. Explain each problem, but do not rewrite the paragraph.”
×RED-ZONE PROMPT
“Write my complete essay and make it sound like a Grade 11 student.”
PRO TIP

“Ask me three questions to improve my argument” is pedagogically stronger than “Rewrite my argument.”
