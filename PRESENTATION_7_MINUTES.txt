SEVEN-MINUTE PRESENTATION ROUTE — TWO PRESENTERS

This is a route, not a word-for-word script. Keep the website open before starting.

0:00–0:45 | Presenter 1 | Project topic and goal
Open with the question: "If AI writes a better essay, has the student learned more?"
State the official topic and goal. Introduce the two authors and school briefly.

0:45–1:35 | Presenter 2 | Why this topic and why essays matter
Use the classroom photographs to introduce the work, not as proof of an experiment.
Explain that writing practises argument, evidence, judgement and revision.

1:35–2:55 | Presenter 1 | Experiment and results
Show the three conditions. Explain the five criteria and the recorded scores.
Clarify: one scored product per condition, assessed by the project authors.
"This is a small pilot. It raises a useful question; it does not prove a general effect."
Only present the scores as your experiment after checking the original work.

2:55–3:55 | Presenter 2 | The balance model and workflow
Show the green, yellow and red zones briefly. Select the green zone.
Explain the sequence: think, plan, write, ask, check, revise, defend.
Emphasise the student's decisions and ability to explain the final text.

3:55–5:45 | Both | Scribe AI demonstration
Move straight to Scribe AI using the navigation. Paste a prepared draft of your own.
Choose ONE focus. Show the short guidance and explain the revision you would make.
If the AI service is connected and verified, show an actual ready-made question,
then the four-criterion review of your own essay and the original task.
Explain that band estimates are approximate and not official IELTS scores.
If not connected, say the AI integration is pending; do not claim an assessment ran.
Do not claim that the project trained or invented a new language model.

5:45–6:30 | Presenter 1 | What the project contributes
Connect the interactive model, workflow and writing coach to the educational goal.
Mention the mobile layout and the principle of hints rather than finished essays.

6:30–7:00 | Presenter 2 | Conclusion and next step
"AI can support writing, but students still need to make and explain their decisions."
Next steps: check the original pilot evidence, gather real feedback, connect and
evaluate the AI service, and test the approach with more learners and independent scoring.

PRACTICAL CHECK
Save the static website folder on the presentation laptop as a backup.
The informational pages work locally; real AI requires internet and remaining free quota.
Do not rely on finding the project through Google during the presentation.
Use the direct URL or the QR code. Leave time for one short, rehearsed demonstration.
